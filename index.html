ABOUT

As a Cognitive Science student at UC San Diego, I specialized in Design and Human Computer Interaction, which ignited my passion for designing great user experiences with accessibility always at the forefront.

Driven by my lived experiences and professional history, today I am focused on pursuing a career in data analytics and am gaining experience in the nonprofit sector, contributing to local communities and populations of children and people with disabilities. I aim to bridge gaps between people, data, and technology—ensuring that insights create outcomes that are ethical, informed, and human-centered.
